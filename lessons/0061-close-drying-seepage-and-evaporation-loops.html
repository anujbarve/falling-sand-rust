Close drying seepage and evaporation loops

A puddle dries faster than a lake. Moisture never leaves its allowed range, and seepage stops at nonporous material.

Previously: Bound accelerated slow processes. Keep that working result available before starting this lesson.

Why we need this now

Rain and boiling were enough for short demonstrations, but an endless sandbox needs water to return and material to dry. Otherwise every rainy year either floods everything or every warm year deletes the lake. This lesson closes several small loops under a stated approximate regional budget.

Your result: A puddle dries faster than a lake. Moisture never leaves its allowed range, and seepage stops at nonporous material.

01Distinguish stored water from wetness

A Water cell is visible liquid occupancy. Moisture is stored state in porous material or the documented surface-wetness interpretation for metal. Absorption transfers an accounted rain/water portion into that state; saturation leaves later hits available for runoff. Because a full water cell and one moisture unit are not automatically equal, record the conversion scale and the approximate humidity policy. Tests should not claim exact atmospheric mass conservation when the model includes a noise-driven baseline and delta decay.

02Move seepage as a paired transfer

For a wet porous upper cell and drier porous lower cell, transfer only up to the upper available content and lower remaining capacity. Apply equal-and-opposite amounts in the selected moisture units. A stone layer with zero capacity blocks this transfer. As with heat, choose whether the pass reads old state or staged deltas so scan order does not make one column drain arbitrarily faster. The lab computes a bounded pair transfer with integer values before the full lattice rule.

03Dry toward a context-defined equilibrium

Exposed material loses moisture faster under warmth, sun and wind, while shelter greatly reduces that rate. Drying must not cross below zero or below the approved equilibrium target. A heat-drying step before ignition may generate steam; that path should not also count as ordinary atmospheric evaporation again. Separate the functions and event accounting enough to test them. A darker visual tint reads current moisture; it must not become a second hidden moisture state.

04Evaporate liquid through exposed surface cells

Only exposed water with air above is eligible under the simple design. A shallow puddle has a large exposed fraction relative to its volume, so it can disappear sooner than a deep lake without a separate special puddle deletion rule. Credit weather humidity as specified, and let weather supply rain from that regional budget plus baseline. Use a hand-built pond for the year fixture before procedural lakes exist. Compare water levels over many seeds rather than tuning one lucky rainy run.

fn seep(upper:&mut u8,lower:&mut u8,capacity:u8,limit:u8)->u8 {
    let free=capacity.saturating_sub(*lower);
    let moved=(*upper).min(free).min(limit);
    *upper-=moved; *lower+=moved; moved
}
#[test]
fn paired_seepage_conserves_and_respects_capacity() {
    let (mut a,mut b)=(80,49);
    assert_eq!(seep(&mut a,&mut b,50,4),1);
    assert_eq!((a,b),(79,50));
    assert_eq!(u16::from(a)+u16::from(b),129);
    assert_eq!(seep(&mut a,&mut b,0,4),0);
}

01

Respect a dry source

What happens when upper is zero and lower has room?

Need a hint?

The transfer cannot exceed available content.

Worked solution

Moved is zero because the minimum includes upper. Add the fixture and assert neither cell changes.

How to verify your result

No negative or wrapped moisture appears.

02

Compare puddle and lake

Why should equal per-surface evaporation affect a puddle faster proportionally?

Need a hint?

Compare exposed cells with total occupied water cells.

Worked solution

A shallow puddle exposes a larger fraction of its volume. Losing one surface row consumes a larger share than the same loss from a deep lake.

How to verify your result

The fixture reports exposed fraction and relative loss.

03

Close accounting

List distinct sources/sinks in the regional model.

Need a hint?

Not every change is a locally conserved transfer.

Worked solution

Include absorption/conversion units, evaporation credit, absorbed-steam credit, rain debit, tool additions, baseline humidity and delta decay. Classify each as transfer or explicit approximation.

How to verify your result

The year report does not label baseline-driven changes unexplained loss.

When it goes wrong

Start with the observed symptom. Change one assumption at a time instead of pasting fixes until the error disappears.

Moisture wraps to 255

What is happening: Unsigned subtraction underflows.

Try this: Bound every transfer by availability before subtracting.

Deep soil dries instantly

What is happening: Drying ignores shelter/depth and paired seepage.

Try this: Apply the approved exposure-context rates.

Lake drains every year

What is happening: Evaporation/rain scales or baseline/delta policy are unbalanced.

Try this: Measure a full-year water budget across fixed seeds before tuning.
