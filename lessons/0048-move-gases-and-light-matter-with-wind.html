Move gases and light matter with wind

Wind is readable without material teleporting. Boundary tests distinguish gas removal from conserved mass stopping.

Previously: Apply precipitation at exposed surfaces. Keep that working result available before starting this lesson.

Why we need this now

A single wind direction should influence smoke, snow and leaves without turning every material into the same substance. Boundary behavior makes this distinction important: smoke can disappear, steam contributes humidity, and conserved powders stop rather than vanish. We need material-aware outcomes at the active rectangle.

Your result: Wind is readable without material teleporting. Boundary tests distinguish gas removal from conserved mass stopping.

01Bias movement candidates, not coordinates blindly

Wind can reorder or add horizontal candidates for a gas or light powder. Each candidate still needs an occupancy and path check. Adding a signed wind offset directly to an index can skip walls, go outside storage or move a cell twice. The simple rule should first attempt buoyant rise/fall as appropriate, then apply a bounded drift candidate. A deterministic strength or probability controls frequency without introducing frame-dependent random calls.

02Define boundary outcomes by kind

The active margin is a simulation boundary, not the edge of the infinite world. A mass-bearing powder or liquid stays within it so the observer does not drain half a lake or lose drifting sand. Smoke is short-lived gas and is absorbed. Steam absorption adds humidity to its weather column. Ash is powder in the material table even though it visually accompanies smoke; do not accidentally route it through the gas-removal branch because its name sounds smoky.

03Move complete state and mark it once

Temperature, moisture, lifetime and movement flags must travel with the material. The destination cannot receive a second update during a later scan or chunk phase. Wind often exposes these bugs because horizontal movement can enter an unvisited part of the same pass. Test a tagged parcel across a chunk edge and count how far it moved in one executed tick. The render loop must not independently drift the same smoke sprite and make physics and visuals disagree.

04Distinguish visual disappearance from accounting

When steam leaves the active margin, the visual cell is removed but humidity receives a corresponding contribution according to the approximate water-cycle policy. Smoke may simply expire. A conserved powder remains in the last valid slot. Log separate counters during a fixture: absorbed smoke, credited steam, blocked powders. Then inspect the visible boundary while panning. Hidden removal at the margin does not prove there are no downstream physical effects; the policy is an accepted approximation that needs testing.

#[derive(Clone, Copy)]
enum Kind { Powder, Liquid, Smoke, Steam }
#[derive(Debug, PartialEq)]
enum Boundary { Stop, Remove, RemoveAndCreditHumidity }
fn at_margin(kind: Kind) -> Boundary {
    match kind {
        Kind::Powder | Kind::Liquid => Boundary::Stop,
        Kind::Smoke => Boundary::Remove,
        Kind::Steam => Boundary::RemoveAndCreditHumidity,
    }
}
#[test]
fn boundary_policy_is_material_aware() {
    assert_eq!(at_margin(Kind::Powder), Boundary::Stop);
    assert_eq!(at_margin(Kind::Liquid), Boundary::Stop);
    assert_eq!(at_margin(Kind::Smoke), Boundary::Remove);
    assert_eq!(at_margin(Kind::Steam), Boundary::RemoveAndCreditHumidity);
}

01

Classify ash

Which lab kind should Ash use at the margin?

Need a hint?

Read the material table, not the visual association.

Worked solution

How to verify your result

02

Reverse wind

Change wind from -1 to +1 around one central obstacle. What must remain unchanged?

Need a hint?

Wind changes preference, not collision permission.

Worked solution

Neither run may tunnel through the obstacle or exceed per-tick movement bounds. Counts remain conserved for powders, even if the final side differs.

How to verify your result

Both directions obey the same occupancy constraints.

03

Credit once

How could a steam parcel credit humidity twice?

Need a hint?

Consider repeated processing of the same removal event.

Worked solution

Removing the source cell and marking the event consumed must happen with the credit. Otherwise later passes might see stale steam or retry an already credited event.

How to verify your result

One removed steam parcel produces one recorded credit.

When it goes wrong

Start with the observed symptom. Change one assumption at a time instead of pasting fixes until the error disappears.

Ash disappears off-screen

What is happening: The boundary branch grouped ash with gases.

Try this: Follow material kind and test Ash explicitly.

Wind tunnels through a wall

What is happening: The destination offset skipped intermediate cells.

Try this: Validate the entire bounded movement path.

Steam removal inflates humidity repeatedly

What is happening: Removal and credit are not one consumed event.

Try this: Make the boundary operation idempotent or atomic in the serial event application.

Explain it without the example

Why is ash not automatically a gas just because smoke contains it visually?

Reasoning to compare

The material table defines ash as powder. Boundary and movement behavior must follow its simulated kind rather than an imprecise visual label.
