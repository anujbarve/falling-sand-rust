Simulation contracts

A compact aid to use after attempting retrieval. This sheet does not certify that these concepts are learned.

Promises before loops

For each rule write its inputs, read/write footprint, conservation exceptions, time limit and one tiny counterexample. Write a headless fixture before relying on a beautiful landscape.

Concurrency

A fixed RNG seed does not make overlapping writes safe. Prove the entire read/write footprint of concurrent tasks. Use safe disjoint borrows or synchronization; do not introduce unsafe access to bypass a missing proof.

Exposure

Sky exposure controls direct weather; air exposure controls contact heat exchange. A roof shelters an interior while a cave wall may still exchange heat with local sheltered air.

Support and events

Connected rigid groups need external support. Flood-fill caps and off-region anchoring are explicit approximations. Particles temporarily own thrown matter; re-entry, caps and serialization need defined policies.

Retrieve before reading

Build a five-cell counterexample for duplicate movement, false support, or heat creation. Explain which assertion distinguishes a correct rule from the broken one.
