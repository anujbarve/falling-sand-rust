Art, display and input

A compact aid to use after attempting retrieval. This sheet does not certify that these concepts are learned.

Three units

Cells are simulation units, art pixels are drawing units and physical pixels are display units. macroquad screen dimensions use DPI-adjusted drawing coordinates; do not assume they equal physical framebuffer dimensions.

Rendering rules

Use the art-pixel buffer and nearest filtering for 2× presentation. Preserve hard edges, coherent flat colours/ramps and the ban on light reflections and bloom. Palette and cloud/UI details still need their specified approvals.

Input ownership

Convert mouse coordinates relative to the actual world rectangle. UI consumes a click before painting. A capped border is a panel, never frozen world geometry.

Visual evidence

Reference assets must be linked and capture conditions recorded. Day/night, underground, shelter and required reactions need motion/readability review in addition to still comparisons.

Retrieve before reading

Draw the same world cell in two differently sized DPI-aware windows. Verify its physical footprint and matching mouse pick without stretching the world.
