Ticks and ecology

A compact aid to use after attempting retrieval. This sheet does not certify that these concepts are learned.

Two counters

step_tick counts executed simulation updates. world_tick counts calendar progression. At baseline 20 TPS, 24,000 world ticks make a day, 72,000 make a season and 288,000 make a year.

Scheduling

Elapsed-time scheduling is independent of rendered-frame count. Pause/reset, elapsed-time clamp, overdue discard, per-frame time budget and residual calendar debt need explicit tests. A synchronous tick cannot be cancelled by an after-tick budget check.

Random slow work

At M = 1, choose 16 cells per active chunk per tick. Above that, use P = min(16 * M, 256); the capped fallback approximates additional work. Repeated picks must not permit same-tick state chains.

Evidence

Use fixed regression seeds and the specified 32-seed reliability suite. A majority threshold alone does not guarantee reliability. Compare visible behavior at 1x, 16x and 64x and report accepted differences.

Retrieve before reading

Distribute 10 calendar ticks across 3 executed updates, then simulate an early budget stop. Show exactly what remains owned by the accumulator.
